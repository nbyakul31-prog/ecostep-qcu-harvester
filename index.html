AR101 Computer Architecture & Organization Integration Hardware Specifications
1. Hardware Interrupt Line (INT0 / Digital Pin 2)

The mechanical foot strike generates a voltage pulse tripping the external hardware interrupt line. This wakes the ATmega328P CPU from SLEEP_MODE_PWR_DOWN to execute the energy sampling service routine.

2. 10-Bit ADC Data Bus (ADMUX & ADCSRA Registers)

The rectified storage capacitor voltage is sampled across a 10:1 resistor divider into ADC Channel 0. The 10-bit quantized digital word is moved across the internal data bus into CPU registers.

3. Energy Register Arithmetic ($E = \frac{1}{2} C V^2$)

The CPU uses internal multiplication and shift registers to compute instantaneous harvested Joules, tracking cumulative student traffic and energy density over time.

4. Memory-Mapped I2C Bus Peripheral Interface

Telemetry metrics are pushed over the 2-wire serial bus (SDA/SCL) to memory-mapped address 0x27, driving the 16x2 LCD display to showcase real-time power metrics.
